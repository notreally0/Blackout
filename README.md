# BlackOut

## About The Game

- BlackOut is a Halloween-Themed archade game based on the classic "Flappy-Bird" game. it have the similler machanics where you are a bird and you pass through obstacles and make a high score.

## Why i made it ?

- I always like messing with the themes of the games and this game actually helped me refresh my mind about making games cuz it had been a long time since i made any.

## Features

- Halloween Theme: Its based on dark and spooky vibes of halloween
- Sfx: have 3 kind of Sfx one for flaping one for scoring and one for hiting an obstacle or falling
- Diffrent Theme as compared to classic "Flappy Bird": as writen earlier it is based on halloween thats why its red moon, the city have no light, its night time, everything is dark, there is ghost at the bottom and the stars are bleeding red too.
